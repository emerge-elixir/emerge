## Implementation plan

1. **Range and proposals.** Decode axis enables, bounded fractions and numeric target declarations on both ingress paths. 2. **Content, chrome, and escapes.** Scroll and clip ordinary content in paint coordinates, leaving layout boxes, host backdrop, tracks and host-owned Nearby in place. Descendant Nearby escapes move with their anchors. They inherit the viewport and explicit `clip_nearby()` clips, not the ordinary content clip. Opting in on the scrollport clips both its own and its descendants' escapes, including ink, shadows and hits. Draw thumbs only on overflow. Share geometry between drawing and installed input. Pure position changes must not measure, reflow or place the tree.
3. **Installed input and controller reconciliation.** Use installed geometry and attachment identity for wheel, track press and captured thumb motion. Handle nested axes and edges, stale frames, remounts and app jumps. Normalize Wayland line/continuous axes once; preserve raw observation independently of element delivery. Coalesce native position updates without waiting for another stage. Emit one `%{x: ..., y: ...}` per changed port per dispatch through its installed route. Repeated declarations, removal and five-second callback echoes do not rewind newer input. Never emit a callback for an app jump or layout resize.
4. **Checks.** Pin literal range/target and resize cases, malformed/refused edits, hidden/revealed nested scrollbars, escaping versus explicitly clipped panels on both the scroller and its descendants, drag/click and echo races. Compare one-shot pixels separately from delivered/captured frames and callbacks. Run native tests, focused ExUnit, full ordinary suite by name, formatting and strict lint. Use fresh private source-matched artifacts. Report presenter checks separately, not as timing or cross-platform evidence.

Scope is scrollable block containers on the retained shapes. Editors, focus/key navigation, general gesture arbitration, touch momentum and other backends are separate ports. Do not accept unsupported editor/focus attrs merely to turn a mixed fixture green.

## Reference controls

Both one-shot raster declarations were rejected before the port (`unsupported attribute 7 in first slice`). On pinned `main`, in a 100px-tall scrollable Column with a 20px leading child and a 10px bottom-aligned child, `(5,23)` was blue and `(5,93)` transparent. Retained matches: the bottom group starts at y=20, not y=90. The native mixed-zone control adds a center child and real overflow; see [S1](alignment.md#text-alignment-and-dependent-sites).

A 100×40 scrollport sits above a yellow navigation row. With no `clip_nearby()`, pinned `main` and retained both draw the scroller's **blue** `below` panel at `(10,42)` and the scrolling row's **green** `below` panel at `(50,42)`. The initial retained implementation showed yellow at the second point. This checkpoint qualified the opt-in rule. The [follow-up implementation](scroll-paint-visibility.md) adopts [#124](https://github.com/emerge-elixir/emerge/pull/124): keep the port's blue panel, but clip the row's green one by default. These are one-shot pixels, not evidence of input on `main`.

At this checkpoint the [headless test](../test/emerge_skia/scrolling_test.exs) distinguished three clip choices in the same installed scene. The row ends at y=45, the port at y=50, and the knob at y=55. Clipping the row cuts the knob at 45 but leaves the port's own panel alone. Clipping the port cuts **both** at 50. Removing the clip restores their pixels and local clicks over the navigation row.
