# Row: split after an independently sized turn

**Historical checkpoint; the [static Size scheduler](size.md#s5-row-charges-and-wrappedrow-membership) supersedes this implementation.** A turned fixed-width child can wrap before a Row shares its remaining width. Checked on x86-64 Linux (default OpenGL build). This follows the [six-profile static transform qualification](static-layout-transforms.md#linux-qualification). In a 160px Row, turn a 48px-wide `el` containing the 48px-wide paragraph `AA BB CC DD EE` by 90°. On pinned `main` (`aef5e42f`), the first fully blue pixel of the `fill()` peer is x=59; with `height(px(20))` it is x=20. The preceding retained build accepts the fixed peer but refuses the automatic-height/fill-peer split. These are one-shot raster controls, not click coordinates or rounded layout edges.

- Prove the dependency on the final declarations. A fixed local width and a non-fill height let the turned branch finish Flow and Reflow without the Row's share. Charge its occupied width, then use the ordinary fill splitter. Do not feed Place back into Flow, use the preceding frame's fit, or lay out the paragraph twice.
- Keep work sparse and reserved. Exercise edits to the paragraph and its peers, undo, unchanged intrinsic demand with different line breaks, and a batch that becomes admissible only in its final form. Keep unresolved splits and WrappedRow breaks refused.
- Check fresh vs sparse geometry, installed pixels and local clicks, including the fixed-height control. Run Cargo and Mix in a fresh, identified Linux build. This follow-up does not replace the sealed six-profile qualification or port TextColumn height projections, inline layout turns or Nearby.

## Result

The wrapped local height is **58.078125px**. That is the width the Row charges for the quarter-turned box; **59** is the first fully blue pixel, not a rounded layout edge. The fixed-height control charges exactly **20px**. Shortening the text to `AA BB` charges **19.359375px**: it also starts fully blue at x=20, but its local click coordinates differ from the fixed-height control. All four combinations of fixed/fill peer and automatic/fixed height match pinned `main` at the measured red and blue pixel extents and counts.

This checkpoint required a pixel-width Row, a turned non-fill with a pixel local width, a height that did not take a share, and no nested split of this kind inside that branch. Its early Flow/Reflow job classification has been removed. The current [constrained measurement](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/layers/constrained.rs) supplies the charge to the ordinary Row allocator; a leading fill paragraph can wrap at the remainder. The numbers below qualify the earlier implementation, not all the newly supported sites.
