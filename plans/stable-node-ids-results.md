# Stable IDs and sparse replacements: implementation checkpoint

The ordinary binary CPU headless renderer and detached codec/layout clients now use **EMRG v10**. See the [algorithm](stable-node-ids-algorithm.md) and [wire format](../guides/internals/emrg-format.md). This supersedes the v9 keyed-insertion checkpoint; it does not requalify other presenters or previously unsupported shape features.

## What changed

- Elixir maintains viewport-local live key and occurrence indexes. A keyed node of the same kind can cross parents or replace the root with its matched unkeyed children. Genuine absence ends an occurrence. Branch/site stamps are exact interned declaration facts, not addresses, search hints or measures. Descriptors lose their last pin only after claims finish. Forward scroll references use the final declaration and callback addresses are compared independently.
- Each update emits zero or one `patch(target, subtree)`. Bare IDs keep whole branches. In a record, `attrs: :same` sends no attribute block; `%{}` clears it. Independently, `children: :same` sends no child IDs; `[]` removes them. A first mount targets `0`; a later full declaration is not a reset. Keep the returned state and use `patch_tree/3` on a populated renderer.
- Rust checks explicit topology, the old target boundary and retained subtree heights before reserving and committing. It preserves continuous attachments and old inputs, and invalidates once against the final topology. Scheduling reads memoized final depths. A box memo compares kind, sizing inputs, scale and settled child sizes; glyph entries compare text and effective strike and share immutable pooled spans. Cache collisions or pressure may miss; neither a hash nor an Elixir stamp proves equality. Width offers, placement, paint context, hit identity and presses are still per occurrence.
- Regenerated the eleven scenario fixture sets and replayed all 121 patches against their cold final declarations. Migrated the twenty external snapshots and two echo patches offline, verifying decoded models and the forward/reverse restoration. The old readers are not in the runtime.

## Behavioral evidence

The pre-change mapped v9 NIF reproduced the keyed reference before cutover. The new mapped NIF matched all six decoded final declarations, geometry dumps and whole RGBA frames, and all eighteen raw click probes. The earlier Row/clip controls and first-slice geometry, delivered pixels and hits also matched. Across versions we compare declarations and behavior, **not identical wire bytes**.

On one capped 120×20 viewport, a 20px keyed card moved from the left 60px parent to the right one. Before draw, `(5,5)` reached the old callback. After draw it missed, and `(65,5)` reached the new callback at local `(5,5)`. A press held across that draw completed on the same attachment at the new location. Separate absence/reinsert controls cancelled the old press. These checks use delivered frames and ordinary raw input, not a detached hit query.

Native cold comparisons covered moving and promoting a kept card, a changed inherited strike, root width from the viewport, different sibling offers and clips, and distinct hits for equal cards. Two equal cards with equal effective inputs shared the intrinsic evaluation and glyph run; changing the inherited strike shaped a different run. Replaying a settled root by ID ran no compute pass. Rejected fields on fresh nodes, overlap, outside capture, unreachable records, excessive moved depth and failed growth preserved accepted state.

## Costs checked separately

- **Wire:** the 10,000-child parent padding edit was **51 bytes**, one definition and zero child IDs. Three attribute edits down one path opened six definitions, retaining the three connecting attribute blocks by marker. Growing each kept side branch from one child to 1,000 did not grow that patch. An intact root promotion is a 25-byte header with zero definitions. Callback-only edits send zero tree bytes.
- **BEAM:** a dedicated trace of the wide edit recorded 10,001 entries, 10,001 occurrence decisions, 10,001 exits, no retirement walk, 1,132,165 reductions and 2,546,481 allocated BEAM words. A 401-node deep leaf edit recorded 401 of each, no retirement walk, 68,477 reductions and 227,017 words (45 wire bytes). Each occurrence decision uses only the old position or a direct key lookup; neither run searches old subtrees. These are individual instrumented observations, not timings or an improvement claim.
- **Native work and allocation:** Linux/glibc interposer positives were `[1,1,1]` for Rust and `[1,0,1]` for Skia (alloc/realloc/free). Initial font/tree admission was `[72449,9785,72174]`. Tested fitting updates, including moving a kept card and promoting it through draw-input preparation, were `[0,0,0]`. One-over text growth was `[0,6,0]`; one-over topology growth was `[0,57,0]`; the following compute fit. This excludes BEAM allocation, raster execution and frame delivery.
