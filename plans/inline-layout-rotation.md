# Static layout rotation on paragraph spans

**Implemented.** In `paragraph/2`, `el([rotate(45)], text("ride the bus"))` turns all three words, their decoration and their click target. The paragraph reserves the diagonal's width **and height** when placing the next span. `Transform.rotate(45)` turns the words and clicks too, but leaves the original line breaks, line height and sibling positions alone. This deliberately differs from pinned `main` (`aef5e42f`), which projected the preferred size but left inline ink horizontal.

## Vertical alignment on one line

**Implemented.** Put `align_top()`, `center_y()` or `align_bottom()` on the individual inline child, not the paragraph. For a line of height `H` and item of occupied height `h`, its offset is `0`, `(H − h) / 2` or `H − h`. This neither moves the paragraph nor changes line breaks, horizontal advance or line height. Each continuation of a wrapped child aligns within its own line. The default is still top.

In **Text turns → Layout 45°**, the 21.12px `We` and `home.` share the 102.05px line of `ride the bus`. **Peers → Center Y** moves them down 40.46px; **Bottom** moves them down 80.93px. The teal diagonal stays put. **Paint 45°** leaves only 4px of slack from the phrase's padding, just like **No turn**. In **Narrow line**, `We` still moves but the wrapped `home.` is already the tallest item on its own line.

## Reference cases

- **One phrase between other spans.** With the bundled mono face at 16 and 2px of padding, an unturned `ride the bus` occupies **119.20 × 25.12**. Its ±45° layout footprint is **102.05 × 102.05**: each dimension is `(119.20 + 25.12) / √2`. In the 164px [regression paragraph](../test/emerge_skia/inline_layout_rotation_test.exs), the following `Z` now fits on the first line; `tail` starts below the diagonal. A paint-only turn keeps *every* detached box equal to the unturned control. White glyphs slope in the signed direction; points on the red phrase route through the inverse turn. An empty corner of the reserved square belongs to the paragraph, not the phrase.
- **Break and align the item, not its internal words.** An axis-mixing layout turn moves to the next line if needed and respects float exclusions. A single item wider than a clear line can overflow; ancestor clips still apply. Unturned and paint-only spans retain ordinary word wrapping. At 180° the extents don't change: both angle tags turn the same continuations around their shared center. [Native regressions](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/transforms/inline.rs) cover right/center alignment beside a float, direct Text, separate wrapper/Text pivots, opposite signed turns, a rejected handoff and inline ↔ block moves.
- **Measure vs. the actual phrase.** As with [unturned inline dimensions](size.md#s2-document-and-paragraph-floats), declared width/height contribute preferred demand. They do not replace the word run or donate a block-height offer to an inline Text. The [paragraph solver](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/paragraph/turn.rs) emits ordinary local word fragments into reserved storage, projects their unwrapped rectangle and advances the surrounding line once. When the Text itself turns inside an El, the El's padding surrounds that projected Text. [Paint and hit testing](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/paint/inline.rs) use the signed planes of their respective owners, not the axis-aligned reservation. No new wire tag, NIF, renderer path or layout pass.
