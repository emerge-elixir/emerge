# Scroll clipping and automatic paint virtualization

**Implemented.** The tree stays mounted and laid out. After composition the scroll parent saves which runs of its child order can draw or receive hits. Both collectors use those runs, without searching for rows. No list widget, fixed item height, DSL flag, layout-on-scroll or separate dirty index.

## Current Nearby rule: test the anchor

The #124 result below is historical: the implementation at `3772e7b` clipped a scrolling row's menu at the port. The current default gates **submission**, not the menu's shape. With the row ending at y=45 and the port at y=50, the green knob wins at `(50,52)` unless the row or port explicitly clips Nearby. With only an edge touching, the whole menu (including its blocker) is absent; one visible pixel of row restores even a menu entirely beyond the port. Port-owned panels stay stationary; ordinary shadow and box clips are unchanged.

Compose indexes a possible escape by the host's border box. Project checks the selected host against the visible scroll windows in their own coordinates. A gate can change even if the selected ordinal run is unchanged. Both output sides use the same gate before enqueuing an escape; sealed scenes do not consult mutable attachment state. [Native edge, nested-window and held-frame tests](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/scrolling/anchors.rs) · [public pixels and clicks](../test/emerge_skia/scrolling_test.exs).

The rounded-corner hide/reveal test also runs under the [native allocation counter](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/allocations.rs) on both admission paths. On the tripled Cards listing at 584×506, the 30→31 and 180→181 px moves still projected only port 17, with no descendant assembly or recording writes. Returning to 30 reused the recordings. These are work counts, not frame times. The baseline and verification sections below describe the earlier checkpoint.

## 2. Retain visibility facts, not a dirty index

- **Place gives geometry; Compose gives reach.** Start with the settled, unscrolled local box. In leaf-first Compose retain a conservative extent of what a branch **may draw**, what it **may hit**, and what it exports beyond ordinary host clips. Account for descendant overflow, glyph ink/continuations, transforms, sampling/AA allowance, shadows and Nearby. Keep these facts in the owner's coordinates, not pre-intersected with today's viewport. An empty anchor can have a visible menu. Unknown reach means descend, never discard.
- **One invalidation owner.** Fold accepted edits first. Feed changed placement/style/content/order through the existing [worksets](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/queue.rs) and [change classification](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/changes.rs). Recompute a scheduled node from its settled children; propagate changed summaries/revisions to their readers, including both parents of a move. Changed ink at *equal bounds* still changes a draw revision. A listener-only edit need not dirty drawing. No parallel dirty flags, visibility queue, dirty-row set or per-scroll sweep. Update the local facts of an edited offscreen row even if no frame packs it; a newly visible **clean** row can be packed from those facts without invalidating it.
- **Make scroll a projection.** Keep inheritable paint/style facts separate from the current viewport intersection. A position-only update changes the port's projection, thumb and candidate hit mapping, not every child's retained paint context. Distinguish projection changes from changes to child geometry or order in the existing workset. Moving within the same set of rows still changes their draw and hit coordinates; it need not rebuild that set.

## 3. Save visible children on the scroll parent

Keep `visible_children` in the tree-owned [scrollport state](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/scroll.rs). For a plain list this is just `[first, end)` in the existing child order; disjoint candidates use ordered runs. It is a view of that order, not a copy of the tree. All children stay mounted and laid out. Scroll range and keyed jumps still use the full ordinary layout, not this subset or mounted Nearby extents. Port-owned Nearby and scrollbar chrome stay outside the scrolling row selection.

- **After placement and ordering:** Use the actual unscrolled child edges and final ordinals. A keyed jump needs the final range; a shadow, transform or Nearby can change a row’s reach. Finish the parent’s selection once those inputs have settled in leaf-first Compose, **before collection**. Use the port's inner window for ordinary ink, hits and scrolling Nearby; use the active-axis shadow guard for ordinary outer shadows. Include a branch if it may contribute to *either* stream, even a transparent hit target or a visible menu on an offscreen row.
- **On scroll without placement:** Use the final coalesced offset to adjust the saved ends against the already placed edges. A small move checks the boundaries and advances only past edges crossed; if none cross, reuse the same runs. Cap stepping: for a distant jump, seek directly in proven monotone edges (`O(log N)` for variable heights), never walk there from the old first row. For nonmonotone or protruding children, skip disjoint ordinal ranges using their conservative bounds; include uncertain ranges. None of this runs per draw or per hit traversal.
- **On other edits:** The existing worksets update the port if its effective viewport, enabled axes, order, placement or a child's possible reach changes. Refit affected ranges on a stable order; replace stale ordinals on reorder or reparent. A child moving into view matters even if its parent's total bounds did not change. A color-only change with the same reach reuses membership. For a scrolling El around a long Column, save the run into the **Column’s** order on the port, keeping the wrapper’s clips and opacity. Nested ports own their own runs; refresh an exposed inner port if its effective window changes.

Both sides of `Output::collect` consume the **same saved selection** in the existing visual-band policy. They can apply their exact clips and per-side tests, but neither searches for visible rows. Skipped rows submit no glyph copies, fitted-bitmap requests or commands. The sealed draw and hit streams do not borrow the parent’s mutable window; input and capture switch only on successful installation.

## 4. Generalize to opaque overlap without confusing it with viewport clipping

The subtree ink extent is an **upper bound on possible ink**; a layout box alone is not. Nor does either rectangle prove that its interior is filled. Leaves can additionally report a **lower bound on guaranteed opaque coverage** (start with the interior of a solid alpha-one rectangle). Combine only provable coverage; never fill the gap between two rectangles by taking their bounding box. Bound the scratch used for coverage; on uncertainty or exhaustion draw more, not less.

A child cannot know what a later sibling will cover. Visit the cached visible children **front to back in actual visual order**. Skip a draw only if its entire possible ink lies inside guaranteed coverage from later draws in the same compositing scope. Emit survivors in their original order. Start within one ordinary sibling group; do not propagate coverage across an unresolved [Nearby band](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/paint/bands.rs), clip, transform or opacity group. Rounded corners, glyphs, transparent image pixels and half-alpha overlays are not opaque rectangles. A covered background can be skipped for **draw only**: listeners, wheel ancestry, capture, and a listenerless front blocker still follow the installed hit stream. List virtualization must pass even if there are *no* opaque occluders.

## Acceptance gates

1. Keep a test-only exhaustive traversal with the **corrected** clip policy. Compare optimized/fresh/patched pixels, target IDs and local coordinates; include empty/partial visibility, variable-sized rows, offscreen anchors with visible panels/shadows, nonmonotone placement, multiline text, transparent blockers, and same-bounds edits to hidden content followed by reveal. Move a branch between ports and toggle its role/pose, then undo; fold two accepted edits before one settle. Check no unrelated layout or range rebuild. Test a later opaque sibling hiding an earlier draw: require fewer draws, then reorder it, punch a hole or halve its opacity and recover the earlier pixels **without editing that sibling underneath**.
2. Instrument the port separately from its consumers at 100 and 10,000 rows. For plain 20px rows in an 80px port, offsets 5 → 6 keep `[0,5)` with **no membership rewrite**; 20 selects `[1,5)`, and a jump to the end selects `[N−4,N)`. Assert the pixels and local hits move even when membership does not. On a pure scroll require zero layout, at most one selection refresh per affected port per settle, and **zero row-discovery probes in draw packing, hit packing and replay**. Bound producer edge/range probes for the monotone fixture by `4 × ceil(log2(N+1)) + 4 × visible + 8` across both streams. At the aligned sample require four row draws plus the thumb; count commands, glyph copies and fits separately. Repeat on reverse, horizontal, nested and wrapped-content cases. A fully overlapping case may visit every contributor; moving a linear scan from collect to port preparation is not a win.
3. Reserve summary, visible-run and scope storage on both [detached](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/wire/patch.rs) and [live](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/wire/publisher.rs) admission paths. Recheck mask/plane and traversal bounds, including worst-case fallback. Require `[0,0,0]` alloc/realloc/free on reserved, fitting native preparation, with positive controls. Failed growth preserves the declaration; `:busy` is neither accepted nor queued. Hold/reject a scrolled frame: old pixels, routes and capture stay installed until the new frame succeeds. No NIF, EMRG, presenter or public API change.
4. Run locked Cargo tests, strict Clippy, focused and ordinary Mix tests by failure/exclusion name against the [320-native checkpoint](nearby-layout-scheduling.md#verification). Check one-shot, delivered headless pixels and installed capture/input separately; for Wayland use private raster/GL/Vulkan compositor crops and routed clicks at scales 1 and 1.5. Work counts are not a latency or scanout claim.

## Implementation and verification

[Visibility facts and ordinal runs](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/scroll/visibility.rs) use actual child edges, not a row pitch. A stable order can refit a single ordinal path even when the total bounds do not change. The [projection pass](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/layers/projection.rs) runs shallow-first on the existing worksets, after leaf-first composition. A scrolling El can select into its content container's order. A paragraph retains each owner's word slice with the aligned site; neither collector has to walk skipped spans to find their words.

### Warm work

The [counter fixture](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/scrolling/visibility.rs) checks 48 warm turns: 100 and 10,000 rows, direct plain/rounded Columns, a horizontal Row and a scrolling El around a Column. The producer probe counts below were the same for all four shapes. `N` is row count; offsets are pixels along the active axis. Row ordinals start at zero.

| Offset, in order | Saved run | Producer probes at 100 / 10,000 | Membership rewrites | Project jobs, direct / El | Packed children **per stream**, direct / El | Commands / hits |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5 | `[0,5)` | 6 / 6 | 1 | 6 / 7 | 5 / 6 | 6 / 7 |
| 6 | `[0,5)` | 4 / 4 | **0** | 6 / 7 | 5 / 6 | 6 / 7 |
| 20 | `[1,5)` | 6 / 6 | 1 | 5 / 6 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 6 |
| End (1,920 / 199,920) | `[N−4,N)` | 22 / 35 | 1 | 5 / 6 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 6 |
| Halfway, from end | four starting at 48 / 4,998 | 19 / 32 | 1 | 5 / 6 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 6 |
| Back to zero | `[0,4)` | 19 / 32 | 1 | 5 / 6 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 6 |

Every receipt ran **zero layout, one PaintContext and one Compose**. There were no new content slots, scroll-range reads, builds or refits and just one selection refresh. Direct Compose read one summary root and zero ordinary child entries per side; the El read its one content child per side. At offsets 5 and 6 the same row receives `(5,5)`, but its local coordinate moves from 10 to 11 on the scrolled axis. Pixel 14 on that axis changes to the next row's color. The counts include the thumb and scroll hits; glyph copies and fit additions were zero. Neither collection nor sealed replay added discovery probes.

Reserved capacity is not the same as initialized slots. On 1,024 rounded rows with ascending keys, the first far jump in the previous windowing checkpoint extended **each** of the Project queue, projection map and material index from 5 to 1,025 slots. Visiting the high keys first, or repeating the jump, did not. The final build makes keys addressable when they enter scroll content and prepares material indices on declaration edits. Unseen rows remain unprojected. On both ingresses, for a port mounted initially or enabled at unchanged key count, the same first jump added **zero slots** to all three maps. Fitted-image and scroll-hit index cleanup also uses the previous stream rather than sweeping capacity reserved for hidden rows.

### Correctness and limits

- [Differential tests](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/scrolling/visibility/content.rs) compare pixels and local hits with exhaustive traversal, including a rewrapped paragraph, floats, empty exporters, transparent listeners and disjoint covers. With alternating 18/22px rows containing `AB` and a bitmap, four visible rows packed 12 child entries per stream, 9 commands, 8 glyphs, 4 fit additions and 6 hits. Fit additions are not distinct decodes. At that checkpoint an offscreen owner could supply a visible menu or shadow. The [current regression](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/scrolling/scopes.rs) keeps the ordinary shadow but excludes the menu.
- Reordering, moving across ports, changing a mount role and transferring the same order between a delegating El and a zero-overflow inner port use the final ownership. A tiny positive box, including one whose transformed edges round together at an unscrolled origin of 1,048,576px, is not proof of a miss. Unknown or fully overlapping reach may require visiting all candidates; the logarithmic far-jump bound is for the certified monotone fixture, not arbitrary scenes.
- [Allocation and admission](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/allocations.rs): after reservation, first paint and far/reverse jumps on 1,024 rounded rows, plus the alternating-disjoint-run refit and packing, made **`[0,0,0]` alloc/realloc/free** on Linux/glibc through both ingresses. Failed growth preserved the encoded declaration. A pinned reader made first-scroll admission at unchanged key count return busy until retry. No allocator interposer was loaded into BEAM.
- [Opaque coverage](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/paint/coverage.rs) can omit a covered draw, not its listener. It keeps at most four guaranteed rectangular interiors and declines uncertain compositing scopes. Scroll selection does not depend on finding a cover. These results measure preparation work, not latency, GPU traffic or scanout.
