# Static box sizing, alignment and transforms

Scope: existing EMRG v10 declarations on retained Linux kinds, following [the still-image qualification](image-sizing-and-codecs.md). Reference: `main` at `aef5e42fc789c396505f85c34810b2ca9c008586`. No new NIF, animation clock, element kind or wire tag.

## Port plan

1. **Check the remaining Size/Space/Align contract.** Keep the existing length evaluator, fill splitter, wrapped groups and El/root alignment. Use asymmetric padding + border, bounded fill, even gaps and a fractional viewport as controls. Tags 28/29 are booleans: pinned `main` retains them in declarations/cache keys but has no conditional layout rounding or font-metric operation for either. Validate and round-trip them; do not invent new rounding or public helpers. Placement in Nearby, scrollers, video and editors belongs to those ports.
2. **Paint and hit in the same coordinate system.** Generalize the Image pose into nested, prepared transform scopes. Preserve each ancestor's clip in the space that owns it, including the special borrowed Image mask. Map clicks back to the original border box; a singular scale suppresses the branch, zero opacity suppresses ink only. Keep fitted raster requests and SVG work limits in device scale. Paint-only changes must not invalidate measurement. Handle paragraph runs explicitly: line continuations are not independent block children.
3. **Keep layout rotation separate.** Resolve authored sizes in the unrotated box, give the parent the rotated axis-aligned reservation, and center the paint box in that reservation. Use exact quarter turns, including swapped offers at a quarter-turned root, and absolute sine/cosine elsewhere. Use the occupied extents for placement and wrapping; distinguish the parent's fill charge from the distance to the next child. Descendants lay out in the local box. Do not feed a placed width back into Flow or read a previous frame to solve a circular offer. Reject unsupported dependencies before publication.
4. **Test the real consumers.** Pin reference geometry and before refusals. Test cold mount, sparse rotation/undo, nest/resize, stationary and moving clips, sibling slots, alpha overlap, inverse-local routed clicks and the reserved fitting-edit path. Run release Cargo tests/Clippy and focused and ordinary Mix tests on matched private builds under the shared correctness lock. Distinguish headless installation from window observation; record only checks actually run.

## Layout controls

One-shot raster export, with the BEAMs and mapped native library identified on each run. In a 160×120 Row a red 60×20 box precedes an 8px blue marker. The marker's first blue pixel is not the fractional layout edge.

| Declaration | Plain | `rotate(90)` | `Transform.rotate(90)` | Both snap flags | `rotate(45)` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Marker x, pinned `main` and retained | 60 | 20 | 60 | 60 | 57 |

All five **whole-frame hashes** match; plain equals snap. With natural height/fixed width, natural height/fill width, fill height/fixed width and both fills, the quarter-turn marker also agrees: **20/152/120/152**. Before the port, plain matched; the box refused layout rotation (79), paint rotation (33) and the first snap flag (28). These nine comparisons do not test input.

- **Retain the local box.** For `(w,h)` at an arbitrary angle, the occupied width is `|cos|w + |sin|h` and height is `|sin|w + |cos|h`. Snap quarter turns within 0.0001°; an odd quarter turn on the root swaps the viewport offers. Descendants keep their local axes. In the 200×120 El with asymmetric padding and border, the right/bottom-aligned 50×20 child starts at `(128,67)`; after a quarter turn its 20×50 slot starts at `(158,37)`. [Raster checks at 1 and 1.5](../test/emerge_skia/static_transforms_test.exs).
- **Separate allocation from advancement.** In a definite Row, a width fill advances by its assigned width. In a content Row with no width to share, use the occupied width. A Column sizes a height fill on its local axis but advances below its **occupied** box. With 100px height, local width 20 and an 8px peer, a 90° turn of a bare fill makes a 92×20 box; the peer starts at y=20, not 92. The 92px share is already spent before even gaps or centering. Fixed vs natural Column, bare/capped/fixed heights, and the bare-fill and fixed start/center/end/even controls were compared with `main`.
- **Where local content differs from the reference.** A turn must not change the `content()` operand of `min(content(), fill())` into the other axis. A local 40×20 (or 20×40) card with enough room became 20×20 on pinned `main`; its `px(40)` control turned correctly. Retained paints 20×40 (or 40×20). The width-capped Row's marker moves from x=20 on `main` to x=40; the height-capped Column's marker stays at y=20. The local 40px charge also leaves less room for gaps or centering: the Column marker is at y=72 vs 92 with even gaps, y=46 vs 56 when centered (retained vs `main`). In an unsplit content Row, both a bare and a content-capped fill with 40px content paint 20×40 on retained vs 20×20 on `main`; both renderers advance 20. This is a local sizing correction, **not** a claim of identical bounded-fill frames. A development candidate also retained a stale 40px Column share after content shrank to 12px. [Both transposed sparse regressions](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/transforms.rs) now compare against a fresh mount, including peer placement and the remaining share.

### Admission and paint boundaries

[The layout proof](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/rotation.rs) works on final declarations. For example, a pixel-width Row can place a non-fill after a turned Paragraph finishes reflow, but cannot subtract that late width to split an earlier fill. Giving the rotated block a pixel height in the **same edit** makes the split admissible. A dependent width used for wrapping needs an independent height before Flow. Pixel-width El/Column sites can defer a projected width until Place. No previous-frame guess or second layout loop. Unproved combinations (including direct turned height-fills under WrappedColumn/TextColumn) are not general two-axis solving. A turned block can change a WrappedColumn break or clear document float bands; an **axis-mixing layout** turn on an inline phrase is refused, not silently rendered as paint-only.

[Prepared planes](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/paint/pose.rs) carry the same local shape and ancestor clips to paint and inverse hits; translation and paint rotation/scale don't change a sibling slot. Rounded continuations keep one span origin and their own line masks, including before the Text's own turn. Outer shadows still escape ordinary host clips: their transform and opacity bounds follow the shadow, not the child's hit rectangle. This is not scroller overlay escape. Zero scale suppresses the branch; zero alpha suppresses ink only. Group alpha applies once on overlap. Nested magnification drives fitted leaf/background requests (60×30 then 120×60, no new request on shrink) and the existing SVG repeat limit, not layout scale or the rotated AABB.

The [existing alignment contract](alignment.md) stands: keep child order, align the occupied box in the padded host or settled group, and distribute gaps after sizing. Tags 28/29 round-trip without switching on pixel rounding or alternate font metrics. The port does not implement Nearby, scroll, editors, video, animated transforms or hover. See [D25](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d25-separate-the-local-box-the-occupied-slot-and-the-installed-pose).
