# Other retained scroll inputs

**Implemented on binary headless and Wayland.** Tab can reveal a control that isn't drawn yet. A wheel, key, scrollbar, mouse pan or touch pan updates the same [scroll controller](scrolling.md). This doesn't add an element kind, input subscription, NIF or wire tag.

## Input contract

- Tab and Shift+Tab wrap in declaration order. `on_press`, `on_focus` or `on_blur` makes a control a tab stop; `on_click` alone does not. `focus_on_mount()` on an eligible control is a mount request, not a request on every rerender. Focus, blur and focused Enter deliver the stored message unchanged.
- Plain arrows move by `scroll_line_pixels` (default 30). Page Up/Down move by the port extent; Home/End go to an endpoint. Paging prefers vertical overflow, or horizontal if the route has no vertical range. Try the focused control's scroll ancestry first. Without an enclosing focus port, use the pointer's winning region; without a pointer, use installed port order. A release or modified shortcut does not scroll. Focus reveal moves the nearest edge into view, inner port first. An oversized box already spanning the viewport doesn't move. A port doesn't pan to reveal its own Nearby.
- Direct touch uses `{:touch, {id, phase, {x, y}}}`. The primary gesture can activate an element; other contacts remain observable. Chrome and explicit drag capture immediately. Otherwise, after slop, try a scroll ancestor that can consume the movement, or a matching directional swipe. An accepted pan keeps its owner and cannot click. Drag phases carry current source-local coordinates; swipes carry local start/end and elapsed milliseconds. Raw observers receive contacts, not mouse emulation. Raw masks do not control element dispatch.
- Only a touch pan can coast. A pause before release, a new primary gesture, loss of focus or replacement of its port/axis can prevent or interrupt momentum. Sample elapsed input time, not the number of display turns. Post at most one unacknowledged fling update; a stalled installation does not build a timer backlog. Mouse pans and thumb drags stop at release.

Reference: `main` at `aef5e42fc789c396505f85c34810b2ca9c008586`, particularly `events/registry_builder.rs` and `events/runtime/{touch,scroll}.rs` for focusability, arrow keys, reveal and adaptive release. Port paging is a retained addition, not a claim that `main` had that behavior. Editor/slider focus, virtual keys, IME, custom key handlers, focus styling and pinch/zoom remain separate.

## Where the work happens

The [focus snapshot](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/paint/navigation.rs) holds declaration-order stops, attachment lookups and unscrolled bounds from settled layout. A separate reader holds the enclosing ports' ranges and controller receipts. Position-only frames share both; replacement can refresh the receipts without copying all the stops. Only edits or geometry changes affecting navigation rebuild the order. Both detached and live admission reserve the banks before acceptance. No hidden-row traversal in the [sparse draw or hit collectors](scroll-paint-visibility.md), and no second layout or invalidation scheduler.

The [display dispatcher](../native/emerge_skia/src/session/dispatch.rs) resolves the **installed** attachment and route epoch. Reveal, key steps and [kinetic samples](../native/emerge_skia/src/session/scroll/kinetic.rs) use the existing absolute input serial and per-axis replacement epoch. An application echo isn't another scroll. On Wayland only a successful buffer installation changes the coordinate mapping: retain each contact's surface position and rebase a held gesture when the ratio changes. A configure by itself isn't motion. [Decision D28](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d28-keep-tab-order-out-of-pointer-hit-packing).

- [Navigation and admission](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/scrolling/navigation.rs): 1,000 stops in a 40px port, last ID `1001` at unscrolled y=19,980. A far jump still packed two children per stream and four hit entries (two rows, wheel, chrome). The end row wasn't a pointer target until revealed. Reorder changed the order reader; a position-only jump did not. A pinned workspace refused a third stop with `:busy` without installing its hit or order. Separately, the [allocator control](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/allocations.rs) counted zero allocations, reallocations and frees across first seal and far jump on 512 reserved stops in both admission paths. This counts native heap calls in the isolated test process, not work or BEAM allocation during dispatch.
- [Headless behavior](../test/emerge_skia/scroll_inputs_test.exs): a hidden nested stop moved the inner port to `0.625`, then the outer to `0.9`, and received a click at local `(12,10)`. Controls also cover default and horizontal paging, tap vs swipe/drag/pan, two contacts, attachments, controller echoes and idle termination. The 15 input/drag/swipe cases passed at seeds 17, 41, 137 and 683. A separate interior-release control saw monotonic progress followed by idle before either endpoint; a paused contact and mouse pan did not coast.
- [Stalled stages](../test/emerge_skia/stage_isolation_test.exs): revision 3 stayed red and routed `:old` focus/press while blue revision 4 was ready. Only after installation did Enter use `:new`. A held handoff allowed one post-release sample, no further samples while held, then more progress after release. The older held/rejected long-list jump and scroll-echo cases also passed.
- **Wayland protocol, separate from NIF injection:** private Sway/wlroots seat on shm and hardware OpenGL/Vulkan. Each checked Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Home, arrow repeat (`24/320 = 0.075` per configured line), lifetime IDs, secondary contact up, cancel, tap and pan/fling. A contact held across scale 1→1.5 released at surface `(10,68)`, installed buffer `(15,102)`, row 9 local `(10,28)` without moving the port. The earlier build treated that configure as a pan. The fixture drives the private compositor seat, not `/dev/input` or a physical touchscreen.
