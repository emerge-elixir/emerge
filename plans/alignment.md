# Alignment on the retained renderer

**Implemented:** A1–A9, single-line F1–F2, paragraph P1–P3, TextColumn T1–T2, [Nearby N1–N3](nearby-layers.md) on supported block anchors and [scrollable Column S1](scrolling.md#reference-controls). Plain and wrapped Row/Column children support [start, center and end groups](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d11-place-alignment-groups-without-changing-stacking). A [direct child of El](../test/emerge_skia/el_child_alignment_test.exs) can override either axis. An [aligned root](../test/emerge_skia/root_alignment_test.exs) stays at `(0,0)`; its own child-placement rules still apply. All four linear hosts can [distribute even gaps after sizing each group](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d12-distribute-even-gaps-after-sizing-each-linear-group).

This is a roadmap, not a selection. The remaining sites depend on other features. Put only one slice in the [parity tracker's selected slot](api-parity.md).

## Implementation rules shared by the slices

- **One layout computation.** Extend [`Linear<MAIN, WRAP, TAG>`](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/shapes/linear.rs). A plain host supplies its one group; a wrapped host supplies one borrowed group at a time. Measure in declaration order, choose membership, resolve shares with [the existing allocator](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/shapes/fill.rs), then place the **resolved outer boxes**. Width is allotted in Flow; height uses the post-width fit and actual offer in Place. Do not rotate the tree, re-break a horizontal line using allocated widths, or feed placement back into Flow. Keep the [early packed-width proof](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d10-wrap-columns-at-the-resolved-height).
- **Compute zone positions, not a second child list.** At most a fixed number of scans of the current group: counts and occupied extents for start/center/end, then cursors to place members in declaration order within their zone. Keep the existing all-leading arithmetic and its f32 rounding. No group vectors, sort, per-child prefix rescans, producer partitions or reordered paint/hit traversal. Cross-axis placement remains independent: padded plain host, or the tallest/widest member of this particular wrapped group.
- **Admit only the slice that works.** Reuse the existing [codec](../lib/emerge/engine/attr_codec.ex) and [native reader](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/wire.rs): v10 tags 5/6 (box), 30 (text) and 37 (boolean). No new helper, opcode, NIF or frame API. Keep absent distinct from explicit leading. Check the final owning kind/incoming site, including kept moves and root promotion, in [batch admission](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/wire/batch/layout_sites.rs). Unsupported sites and malformed values must fail atomically; never strip an unknown attr to make a declaration appear supported.
- **Accept edits → invalidate once against the final tree → recompute.** For a box zone change, invalidate the placing parent and, for an El that also aligns its child, its own placement. A changed resolved sibling extent, inset, order or offer can move a zone even if its members didn't change. Check [invalidation](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/changes.rs) and [pass readers](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/layers.rs), including equal-size and late-width stops. Zone-only edits must not remeasure, redistribute or reflow. That restriction does **not** apply when an even-spacing mode changes fill budgets or inherited font alignment changes an auto Text width.

## Text alignment and dependent sites

**F1–F2 delivered:** on both revisions, white `Hi` in a 100×40 El with padding 4 has fully white bounds `(6,8,8,12)` left, `(44,8,8,12)` center and `(82,8,8,12)` right. The non-left Text takes the 92px offer; a content-sized parent or a row need not supply extra space. The [decision and regressions](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d13-align-single-line-ink-inside-its-settled-text-box) distinguish ink, assigned box and submitted click.

**P1–P3 delivered:** a paragraph aligns each completed line, not all lines as a block. Explicit paragraph Font → local horizontal box helper → inherited Font → leading. The last line uses the remaining band at its own height beside left/right floats. [Public alignment controls](../test/emerge_skia/paragraph_alignment_test.exs) and [D14](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md#d14-break-paragraph-lines-once-and-submit-their-hit-regions-with-the-ink) distinguish line alignment, host placement and submitted hit geometry.

**T1–T2 delivered:** [TextColumn](checkpoints/layout/text-column.md) centers ordinary blocks, carries explicit side floats across paragraphs and clears a non-paragraph block below them. It doesn't import Column's vertical zones or even gaps. The document positions the paragraph before aligning lines within the surviving band.

**N1–N3 delivered** for [supported block anchors](nearby-layers.md). The six-slot [public test](../test/emerge_skia/nearby_layers_test.exs) compares complete RGBA frames and local clicks before and after moving the padded host. S1 was delivered with scrolling. E1–E2 still depend on editors; a decoded attr on an unimplemented kind doesn't count.

| Slice | Status or prerequisite | Observable result and stopping point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N1. Above/below alignment | Implemented on block anchors | Align the nearby root in **host frame width** on X; the slot fixes Y. Host `(10,20,80,40)`, panel `20×10`: centered below → `(40,60)`. Host padding is not a second offset. |
| N2. Side alignment | Implemented on block anchors | Use host frame height on Y; slot fixes X. Same boxes: centered `on_right` → `(90,35)`. Test escape, clipping and panel-local clicks after moving the host. |
| N3. Overlay alignment | Implemented on block anchors | Use both axes: right/bottom → `(70,50)`. Keep front blocking and behind-content ordering; don't insert either panel in the ordinary child zones. |
| S1. Scrollable Column zones | Implemented | With fixed spacing the center and bottom groups both start at the end of the leading group, not at sticky viewport positions. In a 100px Column with a 20px top and 10px bottom, bottom y=20 enabled vs y=90 disabled. A 120px top produces range 30. [Sparse placement, overlap and local hit](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/scrolling.rs). |
| E1. Single-line editor contents | Text input, caret and selection | Left/center/right must use one text origin for ink, caret, selection, preedit and pointer-to-index. Put a click on a known character after changing alignment; confirm caret and submitted edit. |
| E2. Multiline editor contents | Multiline wrapping/scrolling and E1 | Align **each** line including short last lines. A click at the same x on lines of different widths must resolve using that line's origin; confirm wrapped selection and caret after scroll. |

The [static transform controls](../test/emerge_skia/static_transforms_test.exs) exercise the `resolve_core` 200×120 El whose right/bottom child starts at `128,67` with asymmetric padding **and** border, including a quarter turn and display scale 1.5. When states/animated sizes land, repeat with effective font alignment, changing dimensions, and grouped↔even transitions; don't make the six box helpers animatable keyframes. Layout/text snapping, transforms, widget-internal slider geometry and image fitting are independent contracts, not new meanings of `align_*`.

## Done gate for each selected slice

1. **Pin the before case.** Record previous qualified retained commit, pinned `main` result (or “no such kind”), public declaration and a failing pixel, local route or explicit refusal. Use independent literal coordinates/rectangles, not another call to the solver. Keep the applicable old leading/cross, D7 and D10 geometry controls unchanged. Replace a superseded refusal assertion with a positive case at that site and a negative control at the remaining boundary; don't just delete the test.
2. **Two correctness checks.** Compare whole delivered RGBA frames to literal controls and sparse edits/undo to a fresh renderer of the final declaration. Exercise zero/one/many children, interleaving, overflow, asymmetric insets, scale 1.5, resize, changing only an unaligned sibling, kept moves, and multiple edits before one settle. Use a seeded axis-transposed oracle for group policies; trace that only real readers run. On refusal the prior accepted declaration and installed output remain usable.
3. **Verify publication, not just coordinates.** Use a [normal viewport](../test/support/headless_viewport_helper.exs): real raw press/release through `handle_input/2`, routed callback/key and exact local point through `handle_info/2`. Cover a keyed press across a move or reorder, a callback-only edit, visible A / sealed B / accepted C and failed handoff; only a successful submission or valid reuse replaces A. For a window claim capture before/after compositor crops and use actual `wl_seat` input. No injected resolved event, sleep or queue drain; no inferred `main` clicks from reference pixels.
4. **Protect cost and protocol.** No extra child order, per-group heap or reshaping of identical glyph runs for an offset. Reserve old/candidate overlap, detached residents and any scratch before accepting. Extend [`fitting_wire_through_draw_inputs_uses_no_heap`](../native/emerge_skia/src/tree/tests/allocations.rs) for cold compute **after reservation** and fitting sparse edits through prepared draw/hit inputs: require `[0,0,0]` alloc/realloc/free, with positive controls. Include a long sibling group; don't confuse this gate with constant footprint, allocation-free raster/BEAM, or a timing claim.
5. **Qualify and stop.** Under `./scripts/performance-lock.sh shared`, run release `cargo test` and strict Clippy on the six existing profiles (no features, Wayland, Wayland+GL, Wayland+Vulkan/deps, Wayland-all, defaults+bench-diagnostics), extended seeded layout tests, focused and full `mix test` on matched BEAM/NIF bytes. Compare **named** failures and exclusions to the preceding slice (post-TextColumn baseline: 70 failures, 21 exclusions); list repairs, no new failures. Update [coverage](README.md), the tracker, [decisions](../guides/internals/retained-decisions.md) and [changelog candidates](../guides/internals/retained-changelog-candidates.md) as warranted. Retire the completed slice and clear the selection before taking another. Remove the roadmap when its remaining entries are closed or handed to their prerequisite features.
